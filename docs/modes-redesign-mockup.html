What's real vs. new

Verbatim from the app: every theme token, .composer-box / textarea, .med-dest-row + .med-dest-select (reused as-is for Financial), .seg segmented control, .msg / .saved-chip thread bubbles, the .utop top bar, and the .mode-flash switch animation.

Behavior

Default mode on a fresh launch is Entry; the last-used mode is remembered within a session. Research is read-only, so Attach is hidden (matching the app's mode!=="research" gate). Medical reveals the destination picker and stages lab PDFs; Financial is folder-only filing.
